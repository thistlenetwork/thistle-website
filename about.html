Questions

Good to know

Who can join?

Current apprentices: anyone on a Modern or Graduate Apprenticeship in Scotland, aged 16 or over. Aspiring apprentices: anyone thinking about an apprenticeship.

Is it free?

Yes, joining is free, but some activities have a ticket cost.

Why can't I join the WhatsApp directly?

We check every registration to keep the community safe and apprentice-only.

How long does approval take?

Our volunteers review registrations in their spare time, so it can take a few days. If it's been more than a week, email us.

I'm still at school. Is this for me?

Yes. The aspiring path is built for you.

How can employers get involved?

Help us host an insight day, sponsor an event, take a stall, or partner with us on webinars, workshops and mock interviews for aspiring apprentices. Email us.
