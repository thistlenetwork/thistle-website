Good to know

Who can join?

Any current or aspiring apprentice in Scotland: Foundation, Modern or Graduate, in any sector.

Is it free?

Yes, joining is free. Some events have a small ticket fee to cover costs, and we'll always tell you up front.

Why can't I join the WhatsApp directly?

We match you to groups for your sector and area, and we check every registration to keep the groups safe and apprentice-only.

How long does approval take?

Our volunteers review registrations in their spare time, so it can take a few days. If it's been more than a week, email us.

I'm still at school. Is this for me?

Yes. The aspiring path is built for you, with insight days, mentoring and mock interviews.

How can employers get involved?

Host an insight day, sponsor an event or take a stall. Email us and we'll set up a call.
