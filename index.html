Getting involved

Three steps in. No cold group chats.

You can't join the WhatsApp groups directly. Every member is checked first, which keeps the groups safe, relevant and apprentice-only.

STEP 01

Register

A short form: your role, where you're based, and your employer or training provider.

STEP 02

Get approved

A volunteer checks each registration so the groups stay safe and apprentice-only.

STEP 03

Join WhatsApp

You get an invite matched to your sector and area, so the first people you meet do what you do.

Your first apprentice friends are one form away.

Answer three quick questions and we'll point you to the right sign-up.
