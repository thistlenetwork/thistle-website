Getting involved

Three steps in.

You can't join the WhatsApp community directly. Every member is checked first, which keeps it safe, relevant and apprentice-only.

Step 1

Register

A short form: your role, where you're based, and your employer or training provider.

Step 2

Get approved

A volunteer checks each registration so the groups stay safe and apprentice-only.

Step 3

Join WhatsApp

You get an invite to our WhatsApp community, with spaces for general chat, events, introductions, football, run club and more.

Your first apprentice friends are one form away.
