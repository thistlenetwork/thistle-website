Who we are

The Thistle Network ("we", "us") is a volunteer-run community for current and aspiring apprentices in Scotland. We decide how the personal information in this notice is used, which makes us its data controller under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Email: general@thistle.network

What this notice covers

This notice explains how we collect and use personal information when you:

  • register to join as a current or aspiring apprentice
  • join our WhatsApp community or groups
  • sign up for, pay for or come to one of our events
  • contact us, or partner or work with us as an employer or organisation
  • visit this website

How we use it, and our lawful basis

Checking who joins

A volunteer checks each registration against each request to join our WhatsApp community or groups, so they stay safe and apprentice-only.

Lawful basisConsent, given when you submit the registration form, and our legitimate interests in running a safe community.

Running the community

We add you to our WhatsApp community or groups and share events and opportunities there.

Lawful basisConsent.

Running events

We use your sign-up to confirm your place, check payments for paid events, contact you about the event and count who came. If you agree on the sign-up form, photos and videos taken at the event may be shared on our social media.

Lawful basisLegitimate interests in running the event you've signed up for. Consent for photos and videos.

Contacting us about an event or by email

Your email address may be used to contact you about community events and updates. We don't send newsletters. If we start, we'll ask first and every email will let you unsubscribe.

Lawful basisLegitimate interests in keeping members informed.

Showing how the network is doing

We turn registrations into totals, such as the number of members, employers, towns, learning providers and sectors, and show them on this website. Small groups are counted together so no one can be picked out.

Lawful basisLegitimate interests in showing how the network is growing.

Working with employers and partners

We contact organisations about events and partnerships, and reply to anyone who gets in touch.

Lawful basisLegitimate interests in connecting apprentices with employers and partners.

Legal obligations

Lawful basisLegal obligation, where the law requires us to keep or share information.

Who we share it with

  • Thistle volunteers who check registrations, run events and look after the community.
  • Service providers who store information for us. Our sign-up and event forms are built on Zite, which stores your answers.
  • Event co-hosts. If we run an event with another organisation, we may need to share the attendee list with them, depending on our agreement. If so, the event's sign-up form will say who they are before you register.
  • Authorities or advisers, where the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell personal information.

WhatsApp

Our community runs on WhatsApp, which is owned by Meta. When you join a group, other members of that group can see your name and phone number. WhatsApp handles your information under its own privacy policy, not ours.

Group admins can remove anyone who breaks the group rules. You can leave at any time from WhatsApp.

This website

  • No tracking or advertising cookies. We don't use analytics or advertising tools on this site.
  • Saved on your device only. Some features remember your choices in your own browser: the colour theme, your readiness checklist, your quiz answer, your event picks and the salary you entered in the take-home calculator. This stays on your device, we never see it, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Fonts. Our fonts are stored on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your details to a font service.
  • Links to other sites. Our sign-up links go to Zite, and our social links go to LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Outside the UK

Some of the services we use, including WhatsApp, Zite and our website host, may store or process information outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards those providers put in place under UK data protection law.

Under 18s

Our aspiring community is open to school pupils, so some members are under 18. We're reviewing how we look after younger members and will update this notice when that's done. If you're a parent or carer with a question, email general@thistle.network.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you
  • have wrong information corrected
  • have your information deleted in certain circumstances
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • get your information in a portable format
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it

To use any of these, email general@thistle.network. We'll reply within one month, free of charge.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a big change, we'll let members know.

Contact and complaints

Questions about this notice or how we handle your information? Email general@thistle.network.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.
